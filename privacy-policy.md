# Privacy Policy for Quick Sort

Quick Sort does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs only within the extension popup and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. No data is sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Game Data

Game preferences such as language, difficulty, and sound, together with statistics such as high score, longest streak, and answer counts, are processed only to operate the game. The extension does not need access to websites or browser activity for its core functionality.

## Browser Permissions

Quick Sort does not request any browser permissions in its `manifest.json` file. It therefore does not receive permission to access tabs, browsing history, websites, location data, files, or other browser information.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
